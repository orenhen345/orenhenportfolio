Sunset

The movie "Sunset" shows the complex and unresolved relationship between the daughter, Hila, and her mother, Miriam, whose memories and lucidity are torn apart by Alzheimer's.

I made this film during my third year of studies in teamwork with two friends — Sivan and Noam.

Together with my talented teammates, Sivan and Noam, we brought this fictitious life form to life — handling everything from writing, editing, and directing, to modeling, rigging, animation, and rendering.

Throughout this intense and rewarding journey, I learned firsthand the power of teamwork and the critical importance of a structured, efficient pipeline. It's what allowed us to transform a creative spark into a film.
