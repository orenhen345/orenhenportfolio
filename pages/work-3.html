Developed collaboratively during the Game Lab course, this project is an engaging, humorous, and emotional two-player experience.

My role centered on the entire animation pipeline—authoring all keyframe animations, setting up the rigs, and handling the in-engine animation state machines and implementation to ensure responsive gameplay.

The sun is setting, the lights are flashing, and the best paparazzi agency in town is almost out of money…

With only one camera left and two photographers still on payroll, your boss has come up with a perfectly reasonable solution.

Fight over it.

Luckily, the city's biggest celebrity, HER, is out in public, and one good scandal might be enough to save your ass.

Chasing Stars is a competitive multiplayer platform fighter in which two photographers battle for control of a single camera while chasing HER across the chaotic streets of HOLLYHER.

Be the first photographer to capture five pictures of HER to save your job. And beware, she's not as nice and delicate as she seems to be.
